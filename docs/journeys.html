Customer and manager journeys on the Styler site

This page follows one order from start to finish: from the moment the customer opens the site until the order reaches them. The site takes the order, saves it and sends it to WhatsApp; everything after that (design, confirmation, delivery) happens on WhatsApp. Each step shows if it already works in the MVP (the current test version), is planned for the final version, or is done by hand on WhatsApp.

Price quote steps

For products without a fixed price: banners, shields, laser work, vinyl, wedding invitations, welcome boards, mugs, Qurans

1. Instead of choosing a quantity, the customer writes a rough quantity (optional) and adds the product to the cart. MVP

2. The product shows "Awaiting quote", and the customer can still finish the order, even with fixed-price products in the cart. The WhatsApp message states that the product is a "quote". MVP

3. On WhatsApp, the customer describes the size, material and details, and the manager replies with a price. By hand

4. The manager can enter the price on the order in the dashboard, which updates the total in the list. MVP

The manager is not technical, so everything is in one simple dashboard with three tabs in this order: Products, Settings, Orders.

Special cases

Invalid phone number

The site stops the order and shows the correct format first.

The customer didn't send the WhatsApp message

The order is saved in the list anyway, and the manager can open the customer's chat from the order and reach out.

The customer wants to change or cancel

They say so on WhatsApp. The manager adds a private note to the order, and marks it "Cancelled" if they cancel.

The customer never sent the design

The order stays in the list and follow-up happens on WhatsApp. The manager decides when to mark it "Cancelled".

Hidden or deleted product

It disappears from the store immediately, but past orders containing it stay as they are.

Mixed order

A fixed-price product and a quote product in one order: the total includes only the priced products, with a note that the rest is waiting for a price.
